- Admission creates a master students row, student_profiles row, and parent_student_links row transactionally. The profile links to the master by student_master_id and keeps the permanent ID bridge in student_id.
- Parent authentication and student/fee access filter ACTIVE links and scope students by school. Current source evidence does not establish a primary guardian flag or a link deactivation/archive workflow.
