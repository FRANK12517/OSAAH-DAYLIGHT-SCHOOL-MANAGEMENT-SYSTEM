Reconcile Approved Role Accounts

This proprietor-only action re-associates the five approved accounts with this school, assigns one target role each, creates or links durable staff records, activates the accounts, replaces their passwords with temporary credentials, and revokes their current sessions. It removes their previous role mappings. If an account has staff records at another school or a parent-student link, the complete operation stops without changes so those records are not orphaned.

Targets: School Admin, Headteacher, Assistant Headteacher, Accountant, and Teacher (frank.abban).
